macOS
  Double-click "Install Vireo.command". If macOS says it cannot be opened,
  right-click it, choose Open, then Open again. It copies the AU and VST3 into
  your plug-in folders and the standalone app into Applications.

  Manual install:
    Vireo.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
    Vireo.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  then run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine on both.

Windows
  Copy Vireo.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ and rescan in your DAW.
  Vireo.exe is the standalone version.

Load Vireo as an instrument on a MIDI track. Your computer keyboard plays it
too when the plug-in window has focus: A to K for notes, Z and X for octaves.
